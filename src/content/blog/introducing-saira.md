Saira is a product studio. We build a few focused tools and run them ourselves, from the first sketch to production.

## What we make

**Rewards** turns wallet activity into loyalty: points, badges, leaderboards and rewards people can claim.

**Warns** sits between AI agents and their payments, and checks every payment before anything is signed.

**Waken** gives every AI agent its own machine in the cloud, with a memory that never resets.

## How we work

Small team, short loops, and design treated as part of the product rather than a layer on top. Every product ships to real users early and gets better in public.
