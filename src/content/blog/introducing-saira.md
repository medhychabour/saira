Saira is an independent research and product lab building at the intersection of artificial intelligence and Web3.

We believe the next generation of software will be autonomous, programmable and increasingly connected to onchain economies. This shift creates new challenges in infrastructure, security and how digital products interact with their users.

Our mission is to turn these challenges into products that developers and businesses can build on.

We combine research, engineering and product design to transform ambitious ideas into production-ready software, with a focus on technical excellence, usability and long-term scalability.

## What we're building

We're developing three independent products addressing distinct opportunities across Web3 and autonomous AI infrastructure.

### Rewards

**Programmable loyalty infrastructure for Web3.**

Rewards helps Web3 businesses turn onchain activity into measurable engagement and long-term user retention.

By combining wallet intelligence, programmable incentives and automated reward distribution, Rewards enables businesses to build loyalty programs powered by real user behavior.

Our goal is to make onchain loyalty accessible, measurable and scalable for the next generation of digital products.

[Explore Rewards](https://rewards.so)

### Warns

**The security layer for agentic payments.**

As AI agents become capable of executing financial transactions autonomously, securing their financial operations becomes essential.

Warns is building a programmable security layer that enables developers and businesses to enforce spending policies, evaluate transaction risk and validate agent-initiated payments before they are signed.

We're developing the security infrastructure needed to give autonomous agents greater financial capabilities while keeping their operations under control.

[Explore Warns](https://warns.xyz)

### Waken

**Cloud infrastructure for autonomous AI agents.**

AI agents require a new approach to cloud infrastructure. Unlike traditional applications, they need persistent environments, isolated execution and the ability to operate independently over extended periods.

Waken provides purpose-built cloud infrastructure for deploying and operating autonomous AI agents in isolated sandboxes, with durable memory and scalable compute.

Our goal is to simplify the entire agent deployment lifecycle, allowing developers to focus on building intelligent systems rather than managing the infrastructure behind them.

[Explore Waken](https://waken.sh)

## Our approach

We believe exceptional products emerge when research, engineering and design operate as one.

At Saira, we work with a focused, product-driven approach. We identify meaningful technical problems, explore emerging technologies and translate our findings into software designed for real-world adoption.

We prioritize rapid experimentation, direct user feedback and continuous iteration. Rather than building technology in isolation, we validate our ideas through products that developers and businesses can actually use.

Each product operates independently, with its own roadmap and market, while sharing the same commitment to technical excellence and thoughtful product design.
